The Desperation Index

Communities with high unemployment are measurably more permissive toward data center construction. The generative model captures this as an ‘unemployment boost’ — economically desperate cities accept development even when informed of downsides, while prosperous cities can afford to be selective.

Synthetic distributions from generative model parameters. Unemployment data: Census ACS 5-Year (2022). Threshold lines show 50% (simple majority) and 75% (supermajority) consent requirements.
