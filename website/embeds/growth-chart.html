Growth Trajectories

Each scenario starts from a different 2025 baseline: under laissez-faire, all 337 existing facilities (10.2 GW) remain. Under stricter consent regimes, many would never have been approved. Firm investment recovers most of the baseline. The simulation then projects 10 years of growth from each starting point.
